## Backlog Rules

1. Any idea can be added here — no threshold for entry
2. Before promoting to the Pipeline, the idea must have a hypothesis statement in [Hypotheses.md](Hypotheses.md)
3. Priority is assessed relative to the conviction engine needs: P1 items block engine construction; P2 items enrich it; P3 items are nice-to-have
4. Ideas that are superseded by other studies should be marked Superseded and removed from active consideration, not deleted
