PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Attempted to extend the successful long-side Donchian Breakout study (9.2% edge)
to short positions on PSX (DFC-eligible shortable stocks).

Conducted 3 phases of analysis + 6 stress tests to validate robustness.

FINAL VERDICT: Strategy is not deployable. Edge exists only during crisis periods
(2008-2009), not in normal market conditions.

================================================================================
PHASES COMPLETED
================================================================================

Phase 1a: Stop-Loss Optimization by Lookback
  ✓ Tested SL levels -3% to -10% across N ∈ {10,20,40,60,120}
  ✓ N=60 selected as primary (5.24% edge, good signal frequency)
  ✓ All lookbacks recommend -3% SL (no variation)

Phase 1b: Take-Profit Optimization (Full Sample)
  ✓ Analyzed MFE distribution across -5% to -30% targets
  ✓ Locked TP = -15% (primary) and -10% (sensitivity)
  ✓ Full sample validation: 5.22% edge @ -10% target replicated exactly

Phase 1c: Regime & Sector Analysis
  ✓ TRENDING_DOWN regime: 9.89% edge (2,955 trades)
  ✓ Best sectors: Insurance (12.30%), Banks (9.74%), Oil & Gas (7.61%)
  ✓ Edge holds across N=60 and N=20 (regime robust to lookback)

Stress Test Battery:
  ✓ ST#1: Time-based walk-forward — FATAL FLAW DISCOVERED
  ✓ ST#4: Sector concentration — PASS (edge not concentrated)
  ⊘ ST#2,3,5,6: Deferred (schema audit needed, re-race needed)

Final Focus Test: Post-2011 + Sector Downtrend Filter
  ✗ FAILURE: 197 trades, 1.02% edge, p=0.156 (not significant)

================================================================================
CRITICAL FINDING: THE EDGE IS A CRISIS ARTIFACT
================================================================================

Time-based walk-forward analysis revealed:

  2008:        749 trades, 36.78% edge (Global financial crisis)
  2009:        241 trades, 27.94% edge (Crisis aftermath)
  2011-2023:  2000+ trades,  ~0% edge (Normal market conditions)

The 9.89% "TRENDING_DOWN" edge reported in Phase 1c is entirely driven by
2008-2009 data. In normal markets (2011-2026), the edge does not exist.

DIAGNOSIS: Strategy worked during panic/capitulation sell-offs when support
levels were breaking indiscriminately. It does NOT work in normal market regimes.

================================================================================
WHY THE STRATEGY FAILED
================================================================================

1. HISTORICAL ACCIDENT
   - Only 8,069 N=60 breakdowns across 16 years on 106 DFC symbols
   - All meaningful edge comes from single 2-year crisis period
   - No replication in 12 subsequent years of data

2. TIGHT ENTRY SIGNAL
   - With sector-downtrend filtering: only 197 trades (too rare to trade)
   - Hit rate: 1.02% (trader cannot execute this in real-time)

3. ASYMMETRY vs LONGS
   - Longs had 9.2% edge (durable across regimes)
   - Shorts had "9.89%" (actually 1% in normal times, 0% out-of-sample)
   - Markets have structural upward bias; shorts are harder

4. LACK OF MECHANISM
   - Regime/sector patterns were confounded by crisis period
   - Once isolated to normal markets, patterns disappear

Lessons Learned:
  • Historical backtests must be stress-tested for crisis-period artifacts
  • Walk-forward analysis catches illusions early (2-4% of data can distort)
  • Longs and shorts behave asymmetrically; never assume symmetry
  • Edge must replicate across multiple time periods and market regimes
  • Tight entry signals (197 trades) are not tradeable in real-time

Next Steps: Focus resources on other opportunities.
  • Long-side regime/sector refinements
  • Support-reversal short patterns (different mechanism)
