KEY FINDINGS
============

1. TIGHTER STOPS REQUIRED FOR SHORTS
   Sharp inflection observed at -4% SL. Unlike longs (optimal at -6%),
   shorts show peak edge at -3% SL. This suggests short breakdowns are
   "naturally tight" signals requiring quick exits.

2. EDGE MAGNITUDE: SHORTS VS. LONGS
   Metric              Longs       Shorts      Ratio
   Optimal SL          -6%         -3%         0.5x
   TP rate @ optimal   47.7%       4.56%       0.096x
   Control TP rate     38.5%       0.62%       0.016x
   Edge @ optimal      9.2%        3.94%       0.428x

   Shorts edge is ~43% of longs edge in absolute terms.

3. TARGET CONSISTENCY
   Edge declines smoothly from -5% to -50% targets, confirming that
   closer targets are easier to hit. The -5% and -10% targets are
   most practical.

4. UNIVERSE COMPOSITION
   105/106 DFC symbols produced breakdowns. Universe is deep and
   representative, not limited to handful of liquid names.
